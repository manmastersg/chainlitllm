# Support and Maintenance

**Summary**: Information on warranty, troubleshooting, error handling, and the maintenance of Telestream PRISM instruments.

**Sources**: raw/PRISM-MPS-MPD-MPP-User-Manual-D00013488R-cc.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-31

---

This page provides guidance on managing the health of your PRISM instrument, resolving operational issues, and understanding warranty coverage.

## Troubleshooting

If the instrument behaves unexpectedly or errors occur:

- **Check Error Logs**: Consult the **Event Log Application** to identify recent system alerts or failures.
- **Common Troubleshooting Steps**: Verify power connections, cabling integrity (SFP/SDI), and software-level settings.
- **Diagnostic Tools**: Utilize built-in tools like the **IP Status** or **Jitter Application** to diagnose connectivity issues.

## Maintenance

- **Cleaning**: Keep the instrument surfaces clean and dry. Avoid using harsh chemicals on screens or in-ports.
- **Firmware/Software Updates**: Regularly check for and apply updates to ensure the latest features and stability.
- **Hardware Inspection**: Periodically inspect cables, SFP modules, and mounting hardware for physical wear or damage.

## Warranty and Service

- **Warranty Coverage**: Standard one-year warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship (check your specific documentation for details).
