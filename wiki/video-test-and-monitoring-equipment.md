# Video Test and Monitoring Equipment

**Summary**: Overview of the Telestream PRISM family of video test and monitoring equipment, covering various form factors and technical specifications.

**Sources**: raw/Video Test and Monitoring Equipment.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

This page provides a high-level overview of the Telestream PRISM series, including the MPS, MPD, and MPP models. These instruments are designed for professional video environments to support SDI and IP-based workflows.

The PRISM family is categorized into three main series based on form factor and capabilities:

- [[mps-series]] (3RU Half-rack)
- [[mpd-series]] (3RU Full-rack)
- [[mpp-series]] (1RU Full-rack)

Key capabilities across the family include support for various video formats (SDI and IP), as well as specialized software and hardware options like [[prism-options]].
