# PRISM Family

**Summary**: Overview of the different product series within the Telestream PRISM family.

**Sources**: raw/Video Test and Monitoring Equipment.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

The Telestream PRISM family consists of three distinct series of video test and monitoring instruments, each designed for different deployment needs:

### 1. MPS Series

The **MPS Series** consists of 3RU half-rack units. They are compact yet powerful, suitable for environments where space is a consideration but professional-grade monitoring is required.

- Models: MPS-100, MPS-200, MPS-300

### 2. MPD Series

The **MPD Series** features 3RU full-rack form factors. These models provide more space and interface options compared to the MPS series, suitable for standard rack installations.

- Models: MPD-100, MPD-200, MPD-300

### 3. MPP Series

The **MPP Series** provides a 1RU full-rack form factor, making it highly space-efficient for dense rack environments.

- Models: MPP-100, MPP-200, MPP-300

Each series offers varying levels of support for SDI and IP-based video formats, as well as different connectivity options.
